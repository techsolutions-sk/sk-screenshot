# SK Screenshot: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

SK Screenshot is published by Tech Solutions SK. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

## What the app collects

SK Screenshot does not collect, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Screenshots stay on your PC

Screenshots you capture are kept in memory and saved only where you choose (a file, or the clipboard). App settings and your upload history are stored only on your PC, in your Windows user profile.

## Optional upload

SK Screenshot can upload a screenshot only when you choose the Upload option. Before the first upload the app asks for your permission. The image is then sent to catbox.moe, a third-party image hosting service, which returns a public link. Anyone with that link can view the image, so do not upload screenshots containing private information. Uploaded images are handled under catbox.moe's own terms and privacy policy (https://catbox.moe). SK Screenshot does not send any other data.

## Share and search buttons

If you choose to share a link to X (Twitter), Facebook or WhatsApp, or search the image with Google Lens, SK Screenshot opens that website in your web browser. What you do there is covered by that website's own privacy policy.

## Children

SK Screenshot does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
